Microbiome

A chamber of sediment, simulated tick by tick and drawn in 3D with the Strata engine (WebGL 2). Aerobes, fermenters and cross-feeders swim, feed, divide and die over fields of nutrient, waste and oxygen that they change. Nutrient seeps from below and oxygen from the top, so the species sort into bands. Or become one of them, and try to stay alive while amoebae hunt you.

How to play
  • Watch the community grow, or become one of its cells: pick a species (or press F for the cell in the middle of the screen) and try to stay alive. Swim with WASD or arrows, or drag on a phone. Hold Shift or Dash to sprint. Space or Toxin fires a bacteriocin burst that stuns and hurts amoebae and rival cells nearby. P pauses.
  • Amoebae hunt you; a fed one follows your scent. Your species' limits still apply: aerobes need the oxygen near the top, fermenters are poisoned by it, and thick waste near the seeps poisons all but cross-feeders. At 900 energy you divide; if you die, your descendants carry on.
  • Mouse: drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Touch: drag with one finger to pan, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate.
  • Watching, the keyboard: WASD or arrows pan, Q/E rotate, +/− zoom, 1–4 field, Space pause, ,/. slower/faster, Tab statistics, R new chamber.
  • Each chamber is seeded: the same seed always grows the same way, on any computer.
