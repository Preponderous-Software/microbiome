Microbiome

A chamber of sediment, simulated tick by tick and drawn in 3D with the Strata engine (WebGL 2). Aerobes, fermenters and cross-feeders swim, feed, divide and die over fields of nutrient, waste and oxygen that they change. Nutrient seeps from below and oxygen from the top, so the species sort into bands.

How to play
  • Nothing to win: watch the community grow. The overlay counts each species and charts the population.
  • Mouse: drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Touch: drag with one finger to pan, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers to rotate.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows pan, Q/E rotate, +/− zoom, 1–4 field, Space pause, ,/. slower/faster, Tab statistics, R new chamber.
  • Each chamber is seeded: the same seed always grows the same way, on any computer.
